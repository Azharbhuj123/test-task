# Campaign Operations Knowledge Base

## 1. Budget Management Guidelines

### Budget Increase Rules
- Budget increases of more than 20% require human approval before execution.
- All budget modifications to HIGH-performing campaigns should be carefully reviewed.
- Always check campaign conversion rate before approving budget increases.
- Campaigns with a conversion rate below 1.5% should NOT receive budget increases without analysis.
- Budget increases for campaigns spending less than 70% of their current budget are generally wasteful.

### Budget Decrease Rules
- Budget decreases can be performed with lower-risk approval processes.
- Do not decrease budgets during peak conversion periods.
- If a campaign has recent high-value conversions, consult before reducing budget.

### Example Budget Decision
If Campaign Alpha has:
- Budget: $100/day
- Spend: $72/day (72% utilization)
- Conversions: 18
- Conversion Rate: 2.1%

A budget increase to $120 might be reasonable if the campaign is consistently spending near its cap, but requires approval.

## 2. Campaign Status Guidelines

### Pausing Campaigns
- Pausing a campaign is a HIGH-RISK action and requires human approval.
- Do not pause campaigns that have recent successful conversions.
- Campaigns with a CTR (Click-Through Rate) above 5% should not be paused without reason.
- Always confirm with the user before pausing any ACTIVE campaign.

### Resuming Campaigns
- Resuming a PAUSED campaign is considered a LOW-RISK action.
- Verify the campaign budget is still valid before resuming.
- Check that the campaign objective is still relevant.

## 3. Campaign Performance Analysis

### Key Metrics to Monitor
1. **Conversion Rate** — Target: Above 2.0%
2. **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** — Target: Above 3.0%
3. **Cost Per Conversion** — Should decrease over time for healthy campaigns
4. **Budget Utilization** — Healthy campaigns should spend 80-95% of their budget

### Performance Classification
| Conversion Rate | Classification | Action |
|----------------|----------------|--------|
| > 3.0%         | Excellent       | Consider scaling budget |
| 2.0% - 3.0%    | Good            | Maintain and optimize |
| 1.0% - 2.0%    | Average         | Monitor closely |
| < 1.0%         | Poor            | Review or pause |

### When to Scale a Campaign
A campaign should be considered for budget scaling when:
- Conversion rate is above 2.5%
- Budget utilization is above 85%
- The campaign has been active for at least 7 days
- There are no major seasonality concerns

## 4. Objective-Based Guidelines

### CONVERSIONS Objective
- Primary KPI: Number of conversions and conversion rate
- Budget optimization: Focus on cost-per-conversion
- Scaling trigger: Conversion rate > 2.5% sustained over 5+ days

### TRAFFIC Objective
- Primary KPI: Clicks and CTR
- Budget optimization: Maximize clicks within budget
- Scaling trigger: CTR > 4% and high engagement

### LEADS Objective
- Primary KPI: Lead form submissions
- Budget optimization: Cost per lead
- Scaling trigger: Lead cost < target and volume is insufficient

### ENGAGEMENT Objective
- Primary KPI: Likes, comments, shares
- Budget optimization: Cost per engagement
- Scaling trigger: High engagement rate and brand awareness goals

### AWARENESS Objective
- Primary KPI: Impressions and reach
- Budget optimization: CPM (Cost per thousand impressions)
- Scaling trigger: Reach targets not being met

## 5. Approval Decision Guidelines

### When to Approve Budget Increases
✅ Approve when:
- Conversion rate is above 2.0%
- Campaign is utilizing 80%+ of current budget
- The requested increase is under 50% of current budget
- Campaign has been running for at least 5 days
- Recent performance trend is positive

❌ Reject when:
- Conversion rate is below 1.5%
- Campaign is underutilizing current budget (< 70% utilization)
- The requested increase is over 100% of current budget
- There are recent quality issues or policy flags
- The campaign is in a test/draft phase

### When to Approve Campaign Pauses
✅ Approve when:
- Budget is nearly exhausted
- Performance is significantly below target
- There are technical issues with the campaign
- Business requires temporary halt

❌ Reject when:
- Campaign is in peak performance period
- Pausing would disrupt ongoing conversions
- Campaign has active leads in pipeline

## 6. Reporting Guidelines

### Daily Review Checklist
1. Check all active campaigns for budget pacing
2. Review conversion rates for anomalies
3. Identify campaigns that need budget adjustments
4. Look for underperforming campaigns
5. Check for any pending approvals

### Weekly Optimization Checklist
1. Analyze 7-day performance trends
2. Compare campaigns by objective performance
3. Identify top-performing campaigns for budget reallocation
4. Review and adjust campaign targeting if available
5. Document changes made and their impact

## 7. Risk Classification Reference

| Action                      | Risk Level | Approval Required |
|-----------------------------|------------|-------------------|
| View campaigns              | None        | No               |
| View campaign metrics       | None        | No               |
| View knowledge base         | None        | No               |
| Resume paused campaign      | Low         | No               |
| Update campaign budget      | High        | YES              |
| Pause active campaign       | High        | YES              |

All HIGH-RISK actions require explicit human approval through the approval workflow before execution.
